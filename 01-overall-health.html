Overall Health: your 7-day trend
  Overall Health is your average Readiness over the last 7 days — the bigger-picture trend. It smooths out any single great or rough day so you can see where you really stand.
How it works
It’s built from each day’s Readiness score across the past week, and every day counts equally — so one rough shift won’t sink it. (Readiness itself is 50% sleep, 25% nutrition, 25% fitness.) The result is a steady read on your week instead of a reaction to today.
How to read it
  85–100  — Consistently primed, week over week.
  70–84  — Holding solid across the week.
  55–69  — The week is trending down. Build recovery in before it slides further.
  Below 55  — A rough stretch. Prioritize the basics, starting with sleep.
  One off day won’t move it much — that’s the point. Overall Health shows where you really stand, so you can steer the week instead of chasing a daily number.
