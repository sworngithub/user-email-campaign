Accountability & the care loop

Follow-through between visits, with the patient in control.

Most care plans fail in the weeks between appointments. Howard checks in daily, reminds patients of the commitments they made with your team, and keeps them on track.

  • Daily accountability. Check-ins, nudges and progress on the goals your team set.
  • Patient-controlled sharing. If the patient opts in, Howard sends progress data back to your clinic. They choose what to share and can turn it off whenever they want.
  • A clinic view of progress. See improvements, plateaus and early warning signs across the patients who share, so you can step in sooner.
  • Better appointments. Start each visit with weeks of real-world data instead of the patient’s memory.

FAQ

Questions clinics ask us

What kinds of clinics is this for?

Any practice that wants to support patients between visits: functional and integrative medicine, longevity and wellness clinics, hormone and metabolic health, sports medicine, chiropractic, and primary care practices with a lifestyle focus.

Which materials can we provide?

Published papers, articles, books, treatment protocols, patient handouts, care-plan templates and written principles. If your team teaches it, Howard can learn it.

Will Howard ever give advice that contradicts our approach?

Howard is designed to coach from your library, not general internet advice. If a patient asks something your materials don’t cover, Howard says so and refers them back to your team instead of guessing.

Can patients track their blood work?

Yes. Patients can add lab panels to Howard and follow their markers over time alongside sleep, activity and nutrition. With the patient’s consent, those results can be shared with your clinic.

What data does our clinic actually see?

Only what each patient chooses to share, such as sleep and recovery trends, activity, lab results or nutrition adherence. Coaching conversations are private by default.

Which devices are supported?

We’ll recommend a device for your program and handle setup. Ask us about the current list of supported wearables during your demo.

See Howard coaching from your clinic’s own materials. We’ll walk you through setup, devices and patient enrollment in a 30-minute demo.
